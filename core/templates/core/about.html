About Us

Update Academic Care is built on one belief: students deserve feedback that is prompt and truthful.

Many coaching centers guarantee results yet offer no means of measuring them. We work the other way around: each batch has its attendance recorded, every exam is marked and stored, and guardians can follow their child's progress class after class.

01

Small Batches

Batches stay small so a teacher can catch a struggling student early, long before a report card would reveal it.

02

Tracked Progress

Attendance, exam scores, and payments are all logged, so no part of a student's progress depends on anyone's memory.

03

Direct Communication

Guardians never need to guess; notices, results, and attendance are always visible and current.
